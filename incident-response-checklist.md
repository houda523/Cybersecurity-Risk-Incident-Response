# Incident Response Checklist — Fictional Tabletop

## First hour
- [ ] Confirm incident scope and establish an incident lead.
- [ ] Preserve relevant logs and evidence with timestamps.
- [ ] Isolate affected endpoints where appropriate; avoid destroying evidence.
- [ ] Disable compromised sessions and rotate affected credentials using approved procedures.
- [ ] Notify internal security, IT, legal/privacy and management contacts.

## Containment and investigation
- [ ] Identify initial access vector and affected accounts/systems.
- [ ] Check for suspicious mail rules, OAuth grants, remote access and lateral movement.
- [ ] Validate backup integrity and ensure recovery copies are isolated.
- [ ] Record decisions, evidence handling and timeline.

## Recovery
- [ ] Restore from known-good backups after eradication and validation.
- [ ] Monitor for reinfection and suspicious authentication.
- [ ] Confirm business owners approve service restoration.
- [ ] Complete lessons-learned review and track corrective actions.

This is a generic learning aid, not legal advice. Follow applicable organizational procedures and laws.
