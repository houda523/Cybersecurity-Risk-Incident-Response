# Remediation Roadmap: 30 / 60 / 90 Days

## Days 0–30: Stabilize
- Enforce MFA for privileged and finance accounts.
- Verify backup isolation and perform a restore test.
- Review high-risk email controls and payment verification.
- Define incident roles, escalation paths and evidence preservation.

## Days 31–60: Strengthen
- Improve endpoint detection and patch prioritization.
- Run phishing awareness and incident-response exercises.
- Review privileged access and service accounts.
- Document business continuity and recovery dependencies.

## Days 61–90: Validate
- Conduct a tabletop exercise and recovery test.
- Track remediation KPIs and unresolved high risks.
- Review access logs, alert coverage and incident playbooks.
- Update policies based on exercise lessons learned.
