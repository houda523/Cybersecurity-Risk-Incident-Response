# Cybersecurity Risk & Incident Response Case Study

Academic case study using a fictional organization and synthetic incident details. Do not include confidential company information or claim the scenario is a real incident.

## Included
- Risk register template.
- 30/60/90-day remediation plan.
- Incident response checklist.
- Fictional phishing/ransomware tabletop scenario.

## Topics
Risk assessment, phishing, business email compromise, ransomware response, backup resilience, legal/compliance coordination, communications, and recovery planning.
